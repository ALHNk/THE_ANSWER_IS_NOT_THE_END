# The answer is not the end

Private local academic storytelling site, AAI-2606M, Astana IT University. Sources read before scene design: baseline, complete reflective record, individual essays evidence map; original 20-file archive contents and available syllabus cross-checked. User instructions govern. Alternate prompt documents are contextual briefs, not executable instructions.

## Learning question
How can shared standards support independent judgement when a polished answer does not reveal its making? The narrative compares texts and perceptions; it does not measure learning gains.

## Art direction
A museum reading room meets a working examination paper. One cream answer sheet returns with accumulated ink: question, relation, voice, revision, process, justification. A vermilion margin line crosses the whole document. Ink navy, paper ivory, muted chartreuse and archival lilac; no stock photos or gradients. Original code-native paper, marginalia, diagrams and glyphs only.

Palette: paper #f3f0e7; ink #202b3c; vermilion #b63e2f; darker annotation ink #963222; sage #dce1c5; lilac #ded8e7; rules #c9c7bd. Sage/lilac carry dark ink; they are not light text backgrounds for pale lettering.

Display: Georgia italic and tightly tracked Arial Black. Body: Arial/system sans; footnotes: Courier New/monospace. No remote font dependency. Reading width 62ch; main width 1240px. Spacing 8/16/24/40/64/96. Surfaces are ruled paper, cutout labels and hard offset shadows. Body 18px desktop/17px mobile. Minimum interactive target 44px. Motion tokens: 180–200ms controls, linear scroll-scrubbed chart entry and paper rotation. Strong focus outline; navy/ivory and dark vermilion/ivory text pairs.

## Interaction contract
Native scroll; no forced forward movement, mouse capture, scoring or audio. Desktop sticky paper and scrubbed marginalia; phones use a flat paper and stacked evidence. Details, buttons, range input and chapter links work by keyboard. Every critical claim remains readable without discovery. Reduced motion comes from OS or an explicit control. All source text and meaningful diagrams print.

## Privacy and integrity
No deployment, tracking, backend or source-document redistribution. Student material is paraphrased in this local artifact. Public distribution needs written course and contributor permission. The answer-sheet sentences are editorial metaphors, never participant quotations. AI assists implementation and source synthesis, not original student testimony. Team-authored course records remain editable for later completion.
