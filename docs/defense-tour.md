## Q&A route

Open **Q&A** in the header; Escape closes and returns focus. Answer as: claim → source → interpretation → limit → practical implication.

- **Are outcomes the enemy?** No. Biggs provides coherence; Didaktik asks about purpose and situated judgement. We assess reasons without imposing one conclusion.
- **Why N=22?** It is the supplied survey, not a representative sample. We cannot infer causality or statistical significance; raw data and Item 8 are unavailable.
- **Are five people enough?** For situated reflective comparison, not generalisation. There are five authors, not twenty independent notes/participants.
- **Is AI forbidden?** No. Distinguish assistance from assessed intellectual responsibility. Explain decisions and disclose support.
- **What remains uncertain?** Whether the proposed sequence improves actual learning; teacher workload; consistency; speaking anxiety; how students use feedback.
- **What did AI do here?** Research organisation, copy synthesis, design, code and test authoring. It did not supply participant testimony or survey data. Refer to credits and the academic source trail.
