# Storyboard

The same answer sheet appears first closed and last annotated. A continuous vermilion margin and growing seven-mark index connect all acts. The visual object changes in a sticky sidebar as the reader crosses chapters; content remains in normal document flow.

| Act | Dramatic turn | Evidence and reader action | Phone / print fallback |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Introduction | A perfect answer has no visible reasoning | Open the margin question; purpose and textual evolution from Week 3 reform to judgement | Flat paper, visible question |
| 2 Literature | Standards need a purpose | Expand three connected concepts; compare Didaktik and alignment; inspect four survey bars | Stacked concepts, labelled SVG and data table |
| 3 Reflective notes | Five voices complicate one answer | Five member dossiers with starting point, doubt, revised interpretation and exact files/pages | All five summaries visible; native details |
| 4 Peer feedback | A judgement becomes usable through response | Tap before/revised text, keyboard range wipe; optional editable team record | Both versions printed, native fields excluded |
| 5 Poster development | Communication is a design decision | Process lane exposes documented source selection and actual artifact decisions; future rehearsal labelled prospective | Vertical lane |
| 6 Assessment | A finished product is only one kind of evidence | Choose evidence; see explanation and trade-off, no grade | All explanations printed |
| 7 Critical conclusion | The answer returns with its intellectual history | Toggle annotations; concrete course intervention and limits | Full annotations and seven sections |

Opening, literature and ending form the initial vertical slice. The remaining chapters extend the same margin, paper and annotation vocabulary. Nothing requires precise timing.
